What is an operator?
Operator is a symbol which will perform certain operation.
For example,if we take a simple expression, 4+5=9.
Here 4 and 5 are 'operands' and '+' is called the operator.
Typescript consists of different of operators that are used to perform different operations.
Types of JavaScript Operations.
Arithmetic Operator.
Assignment Operator.
Relational/ Comparison Operator.
Logical Operator.
